# Emotion-Recognition
# ✦ Emotia

### Three Signals. One Emotional Story.

Emotia is a multimodal emotion recognition platform that analyzes **facial expressions, voice, and text** to understand a user's emotional state.

Unlike traditional emotion recognition systems that focus on a single modality and a fixed set of basic emotions, Emotia combines multiple emotional signals to identify **emotional agreement, conflicting signals, and mixed emotions**.

---

## 🌟 Why Emotia?

Human emotions are complex.

A person may:

- Smile while feeling sad
- Sound happy while their facial expression appears sad
- Say "I'm fine" while their voice and face suggest otherwise
- Experience multiple emotions at the same time

Traditional emotion recognition systems often analyze these signals independently.

**Emotia takes a different approach.**

It analyzes:

> 👤 What I See  
> 🎙 What I Hear  
> 📝 What I Read  

and combines them to understand:

> ✦ One Emotional Story
