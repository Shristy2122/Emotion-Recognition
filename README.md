# Emotion-Recognition
# ✦ EMOTIA

## Three Signals. One Emotional Story.

Emotia is a multimodal AI-based emotion recognition platform designed to understand emotions using **facial expressions, voice, and text**.

Unlike conventional emotion recognition systems that analyze a single modality or classify only a fixed set of basic expressions, Emotia combines multiple emotional signals and attempts to understand **how those signals relate to each other**.

For example, a person may look sad but sound happy, or say that they are fine while their facial expression and voice suggest otherwise.

Emotia is designed to capture these situations through:

> 👤 What I See  
> 🎙 What I Hear  
> 📝 What I Read  

and transform them into:

> ✦ One Emotional Story

# 🌟 Overview

Human emotions are complex and cannot always be accurately represented by a single expression or signal.

A person can:

- Smile while feeling sad.
- Speak positively while appearing emotionally distressed.
- Say "I'm fine" while their tone suggests otherwise.
- Experience happiness and sadness simultaneously.
- Change emotional state throughout a conversation.

Traditional emotion recognition approaches often analyze individual modalities independently.

Emotia explores a **multimodal approach**, where facial expressions, vocal characteristics, and textual meaning are analyzed together.

The goal is not simply:

> "What emotion is detected?"

but rather:

> "What does each signal suggest, and what emotional story do they tell together?"

# ❗ Problem Statement

Most basic emotion recognition systems have one or more limitations:

- They focus on only one modality.
- They treat emotion as a single fixed label.
- They often rely on a limited set of predefined expressions.
- They may ignore disagreement between different emotional signals.
- They provide limited explanation for their predictions.
- They may not represent emotional changes during a session.

Facial Expression → Sad

Voice → Happy
